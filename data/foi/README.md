Home Office Freedom of Information workbooks with certificates of sponsorship per sponsor.
WhatDoTheyKnow doesn't serve these files to GitHub's servers, so copies are kept here and
`jobscraper/sponsor_registers.py` reads them by the "file" name in `UK_COS_SOURCES`.

- `FOI 2025 12841 Annex.xlsx`: FOI 2025/12841, every sponsor, 2021-2024.
  Source: https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_certificate_of_spons_2
